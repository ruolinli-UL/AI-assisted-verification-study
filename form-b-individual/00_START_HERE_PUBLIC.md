## Individual diagnostic protocol

- Use a **fresh extracted Form B folder** on a separate computer.
- Review the requirements and Java implementation, and run or extend the supplied tests.
- Do not consult a human collaborator, Copilot, or another AI assistant.
- Record the final independent release-readiness decision, rationale, and confidence.
- Keep tests that fail and any implementation modifications in the submitted working copy.
- Form B was **not screen-recorded** in the study.
- The task duration was approximately 10–15 minutes, as announced by the researcher.

## Condition-specific protocol

- Independent inspection: individually inspect requirements and files without AI or other people.
- Snapshot 1: record a private initial release-readiness judgement.
- Collaboration: work on the participant computer using the study-provided Microsoft Copilot chatbot in conversational mode; screen recording was instructed.
- Snapshot 2: pause Copilot and independently record another private judgement.
- Joint decision: resume Copilot briefly and complete `ReleaseDecision.md`.
- In the original study, participants submitted a coded copy of their work to the researcher.

## Files supplied

See `01_REQUIREMENTS.md`, the Java implementation and visible tests, `ci_report.txt`, and `ReleaseDecision.md`. Survey instruments and upload endpoints are not included in this public release.

You will complete this form twice. Complete Snapshot 1 after your initial individual inspection, before beginning work with your assigned collaborator. Complete Snapshot 2 during the collaborative task when instructed by the researcher. Each time, complete the form privately and do not discuss your answers with your collaborator before submitting.

# D. Post-task Questionnaire

**Source:** Ethics application, Section 6.2, pp. 23–24. **Administration:** Microsoft Forms after both the collaborative task and individual diagnostic task.

## Instructions shown to participants

Complete this questionnaire after both the collaborative task and the individual diagnostic task.

### Q1 — Required; single-line text

Please enter the participant code provided by the research team. Do not enter your name or student number.

### Q2 — Required; single choice

Compared with the collaborative task, how difficult did you find the individual task?

- Much easier
- Somewhat easier
- About the same
- Somewhat more difficult
- Much more difficult

### Q3 — Required; single choice

To what extent did your collaborator influence what evidence you sought, including what you inspected and what tests you created or ran?

- 1 - Not at all
- 2 - Slightly
- 3 - Moderately
- 4 - Substantially
- 5 - Very substantially

### Q4 — Required; single choice

To what extent did your collaborator influence how you interpreted the evidence and reached your release judgement?

- 1 - Not at all
- 2 - Slightly
- 3 - Moderately
- 4 - Substantially
- 5 - Very substantially

### Q5 — Required; multi-line text

Describe one significant contribution from your collaborator and how you handled it (for example, accepted, modified, questioned or rejected it). If there was no such contribution, please write “none”.

### Q6 — Required; multi-line text

Did anything from the collaborative task influence how you approached the subsequent individual task? Please explain briefly, or write “none”.

### Q7 — Optional; multi-line text

Is there anything else you would like to tell us about your experience of the study?

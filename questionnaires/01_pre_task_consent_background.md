# A. Participant information, consent and pre-task questionnaire

**Source:** Ethics application, Section 6.2, pp. 19–21. **Administration:** Microsoft Forms, gated by a participant information sheet and mandatory consent question. The participant information sheet itself is not reproduced here.

**Branching:** The pre-task questionnaire is shown only if the participant selects **Yes** to the consent question. Selecting **No** ends the form.
